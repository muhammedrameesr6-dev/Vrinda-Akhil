## Features Included
- **Opening Curtain Gate Interaction**: Interactive gate opening animation upon tap with ambient music and falling petal burst effect.
- **Background Music**: Ambient wedding song with floating mute/unmute control.
- **Hero & Live Countdown**: Elegant hero banner and live flip countdown timer ticking down to December 2, 2026 at 10:30 AM IST.
- **Interactive Photo Card**: Tap-to-reveal framed photo with 3D tilt effects on desktop hover.
- **Venue & Google Maps**: One-click Google Maps navigation directly to Grace Convention Centre, Mavelikara.
- **Family Blessings**: Groom and Bride family details and addresses.
- **Lightbox Gallery**: Fullscreen image preview modal.
- **Mobile Responsive**: Fully optimized for mobile phones, tablets, and desktop displays.

## Replacing Photos
To use your own photos, simply replace the files inside the `assets/` folder with your own images (keeping the same filenames or updating paths in `index.html`):
- `assets/hero.jpg` — Main hero background photo
- `assets/gallery-1.jpg` to `gallery-4.jpg` — Section banners and tap-to-reveal couple photo
- `assets/music.mp3` — Background wedding audio track
- `og.jpg` — WhatsApp / social media link preview image
